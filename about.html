BUILT IN OUR
OWN WORKSHOP

Furn started with a simple frustration: good furniture cost three times what it should, and most of that went to everyone except the people making it.

So we cut the middle out. We buy hardwood direct, cut and upholster on site, and sell straight to the people who will live with it.

WHAT WE STAND BEHIND

KILN-DRIED HARDWOOD

Every frame is beech or oak, kiln-dried so it will not warp as your home changes through the year.

HAND-TIED SPRINGS

Eight-way hand-tied suspension in every sofa and armchair. Slower to build, decades longer to sag.

NATURAL UPHOLSTERY

Wool, linen and cotton, chosen because they wear in rather than out.
